Terms of use

Notes personal Gmail reminder integration · Updated 22 October 2026

This integration is configured for the operator’s personal use. It is an experimental aid for reviewing new email and creating personal reminders, not a public email or notification service.

Delivery and accuracy

AI classification can miss a time-sensitive message or raise an unnecessary alert. Polling, network access, note synchronization and Android scheduling can delay or prevent notifications. Do not rely on this integration for emergencies or critical deadlines.

Account control

Only connect an account you are authorized to access. Google consent grants the checker read-only Gmail access; the operator can revoke that access or stop the checker at any time. Google, the configured AI service, GitHub and the website host apply their own service terms and account policies.

Data practices

Read the privacy policy before connecting an account. Completing or deleting a reminder does not necessarily remove historical copies from assistant history, Git history or backups. The operator controls their configuration and retained personal data.
