Notes

Personal notes and time-sensitive Gmail reminders

Notes is a personal note-taking application. Its optional Gmail integration helps its operator check email less often by identifying new messages that need attention before their next planned inbox check.

How the Gmail integration works

After the operator signs in with Google and grants read-only Gmail access, a private background checker periodically reviews new email and relevant thread context. A configured AI assistant assesses whether an action is time-sensitive. When appropriate, it writes a short reminder into the operator’s private Notes vault.

The Gmail connection reads mail. It does not send, delete, archive, change labels or mark messages as read. Existing mail is baselined at setup rather than generating retrospective alerts. The integration is configured for the operator’s personal use; this page does not offer a public sign-up service.

Your information and control

Email context is processed on the operator’s private server and by their configured AI service. Reminder summaries are stored in a private GitHub-backed note vault and synced to the operator’s devices. Read the privacy policy for the data flow, retention and revocation details.

Reminders are an experimental convenience and cannot guarantee timely delivery. Please read the terms of use.
