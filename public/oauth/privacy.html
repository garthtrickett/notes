Privacy policy

Notes personal Gmail reminder integration · Updated 22 October 2026

Purpose and account access

This integration is configured for its operator’s personal use. Google OAuth authorizes read-only Gmail access. The checker uses account identity, mailbox change identifiers, message identifiers, sender and subject headers, timestamps, email text and recent thread context to identify messages needing attention before the next planned inbox check.

It does not send mail, delete or archive messages, change labels, or mark messages as read. It excludes spam, trash and drafts from triage, does not download attachments, and does not follow email links or load external images.

Processing and sharing

The checker runs on a private server controlled by the operator. Relevant email excerpts are provided to the operator’s configured AI assistant and model service for classification and cancellation/reply review. That service’s account settings and processing/retention policies also apply.

When an alert qualifies, a minimal sender/action/deadline summary is written to the operator’s private GitHub-backed Notes vault and synced to their Notes clients. The source conversation is not published on this website. The integration does not sell Google account data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train models. Its use of email content is limited to personal email triage and reminder creation.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens, mailbox progress and pending processing files are stored privately on the operator’s server with restricted filesystem permissions. Raw helper review/decision files are removed after successful processing; failed work remains recoverable. Managed summaries and message identifiers are retained to suppress duplicates and preserve user edits.

Email excerpts reviewed by the AI assistant may also remain in its conversation history. Notes reminders can remain in local device caches, private vault Git history and backups after a task is completed or its current note is deleted. This integration does not promise immediate removal of every historical copy.

Revoking access and removing data

The operator can stop future Gmail access by revoking this app through Google Account connections and stopping the checker. Revocation does not automatically delete existing reminders or retained history. The operator controls removal of private server state, relevant assistant history, vault notes, Git history, device copies and backups.

Public pages and contact

These informational pages contain no mailbox content, private notes or OAuth tokens and load no analytics scripts. The website host may keep ordinary request logs. For questions, contact the operator using the support email shown on the Google consent screen. Do not post private emails or credentials to public issue trackers.
